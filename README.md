# Serums Expended

Adds Refined and Advanced upgrades for Anomaly serums, plus Marksman, Regenesis and Neuroplastic serums.

- Upgrade existing doses or craft from raw materials at the serum lab. One recipe handles both routes.
- Regenesis repairs permanent injuries; its advanced version can regrow missing biological parts.
- Neuroplastic grants a Major Passion; its advanced version lets you choose the skill.
- Adjust each serum's effects and duration in Mod options.

Requires **RimWorld 1.6 or 1.7, Anomaly and Harmony**. Includes optional integrations for Vanilla Skills Expanded, VRE Android and VAE Insanity.

**Manually tested in-game by the author.** Both versioned assemblies compile successfully.
